Rules

  • Rule 1: Pawns can only move forward one square at a time

  • Rule 2: Pawns capture diagonally.

  • Rule 3: Kings can move one square in any direction.

  • Rule 4: When a pawn reaches the opposite end of the board and cannot be captured,you win the game
  • Rule 5: If your king is in check, you MUST move your king

Tips

  • Move the king to the center to help your pawns!

Engine may take a few seconds to move :)
